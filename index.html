The Story

Where It All Began.

The shop started in the garage of our home and eventually grew into its own dedicated workspace. Officially established in October 2023, Blackburn Concepts became the place where Francisco designs, fabricates, welds, cuts, reshapes, and creates custom automotive solutions completely tailored to each project.

Every vehicle that enters the shop represents a different challenge. Rather than following shortcuts or generic solutions, Blackburn Concepts approaches each build with a fabrication-first mindset focused on quality, function, and long-term reliability.

"Quality takes time. Shortcuts cost more."
— Francisco Vázquez, Blackburn Concepts

The Philosophy

Built With Intention.

The philosophy behind Blackburn Concepts is simple: do things properly, build with intention, and create work that reflects genuine craftsmanship.

01 / Approach

Fabrication First

Rather than reaching for bolt-on parts, we fabricate solutions designed specifically for each project. Custom is the default, not the exception.

02 / Standard

No Shortcuts

Every weld, every cut, every fitment is done correctly — the first time. Quality work takes time, and we refuse to compromise that standard for speed.

03 / Detail

Obsessive Precision

The details nobody notices are the ones we care about most. From first measurement to final assembly, precision is non-negotiable at every step.

04 / Function

Built to Perform

Aesthetics follow function. Every fabricated component is engineered to work properly under real-world conditions — not just look good on a lift.

05 / Trust

Full Transparency

We communicate every step of the process. No surprises, no hidden work — just honest builds with honest timelines and honest results.

06 / Legacy

Pride in the Work

The goal is always to create something better than what existed before. Work that stands behind the Blackburn Concepts name for years to come.

How We Work

The Process

From first conversation to final delivery — every project follows a deliberate, craftsmanship-focused process designed to get it right, not just get it done.

01

Consultation

Understanding the client's vision, needs, and fabrication goals. We listen before we cut anything — every detail of the project brief is accounted for before work begins.

02

Design & Planning

Custom fabrication strategy, measurements, engineering considerations, and complete build planning. Every problem anticipated before fabrication starts.

03

Fabrication & Build

Precision fabrication, welding, fitting, and hands-on craftsmanship. Measured, cut, welded, fitted, refined, and done properly. Every time.

04

Delivery & Finish

Testing, finishing, refinement, and project completion. The vehicle leaves better than it arrived — without exception. That's the Blackburn standard.

About Blackburn Concepts — Custom Automotive Fabrication Toronto

Blackburn Concepts is a custom automotive fabrication shop located at 325 Weston Rd Unit D3, Toronto, Ontario, Canada (postal code M6N 3R3). Founded in October 2023 by Francisco Vázquez, owner and lead fabricator.

Services Offered

  • Custom automotive fabrication — one-off components, brackets, structural modifications
  • Engine swaps and transmission conversions — LS swaps, K-series builds, custom fitment
  • TIG welding and MIG welding — precision welds, metal shaping, structural reinforcement
  • Custom exhaust fabrication — headers, downpipes, Y-pipes, X-pipes, full systems
  • Classic car restoration — body panels, rust repair, structural fabrication
  • Performance upgrades — cooling, fuel systems, suspension
  • Full and partial custom builds

Notable Projects

  • Porsche 911 LS supercharged engine swap — complete custom drivetrain conversion
  • 1968 Camaro restoration — full bodywork and structural fabrication
  • The Blackburn Concepts Jeep — signature custom build
  • K-series turbo Honda build
  • Custom exhaust fabrication projects

Service Area

Toronto, Greater Toronto Area (GTA), Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Mississauga, Brampton, and surrounding Ontario communities.
